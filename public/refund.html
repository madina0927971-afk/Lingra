Refund Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

✅ We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all new subscriptions. No questions asked.

1. Eligibility for Refund

You are eligible for a full refund if:

  • You request a refund within 7 days of your initial subscription purchase
  • You have not completed more than 50% of available lessons in any subscribed language
  • This is your first subscription purchase (not a renewal)

2. Non-Refundable Cases

⚠️ Refunds are NOT available if:

  • More than 7 days have passed since the purchase date
  • You have completed more than 50% of lessons in a subscribed language
  • The request is for a subscription renewal (not the initial purchase)
  • The account has been suspended for violation of our Terms of Service

3. Subscription Cancellations

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings or by contacting us. When you cancel:

  • Your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period
  • You will not be charged for the next billing cycle
  • Access to paid content continues until the subscription expires
  • No partial refunds are given for unused days in the current period

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email us at lingra.app@gmail.com with:

  • Your account email address
  • Date of purchase
  • Reason for refund (optional but helpful)

We will process your request within 3-5 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

5. Technical Issues

If you experience technical problems that prevent you from using Lingra, please contact us at lingra.app@gmail.com before requesting a refund. We will do our best to resolve the issue promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be communicated via email or on our website.

7. Contact

For refund requests or questions: lingra.app@gmail.com

We aim to respond within 24 hours on business days.
